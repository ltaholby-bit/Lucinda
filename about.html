About

The kitchen behind the cakes

We are a small studio. That is the whole business model, and it is deliberate.

How we work

Small batches, no shortcuts

We take a limited number of orders each week. It means we can bake sponges the day before finishing, make buttercream fresh, and give every cake the attention it needs rather than rushing it out of the door.

Everything is made from scratch — real butter, real vanilla, fruit that is in season. There are no premixes and nothing sits in a freezer waiting for an order.

What you can expect

An honest conversation first

If a design will not travel well, or a flavour combination will not work at the size you need, we will say so and suggest something better. We would rather talk you out of the wrong cake than hand over one we are not proud of.

Quotes are firm before you commit. Nothing is confirmed until you have seen the design and the price and said yes.
