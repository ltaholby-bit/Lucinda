FAQs

Asked most often

How far ahead should I book?

Cakes need about two weeks, tiered cakes about six. Decor setups need three to four weeks. If your date is sooner, ask anyway — we sometimes have space.

What size cake do I need?

Our size guide shows approximate servings for every size, from a 4‑inch bento (2–4 people) to a three-tier cake (60–100+ people). If you are unsure, choose ‘I’m not sure’ and we will recommend the right size for your guest count.

Can I send pictures of what I like?

Yes, and we encourage it. You can upload up to three images with any enquiry. They are used as a creative reference — your final design will be our own and may differ.

Do you do cake and decor together?

Often, and it is usually the better result. Choose ‘Cake and decor’ on the enquiry form and we will quote both together.

How do I confirm a booking?

Once you have approved the design direction and the quote, a deposit secures your date. The balance is due before collection or setup.
