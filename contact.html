FAQs

Asked most often

How far ahead should I order?

Bento cakes need 3–5 days. Single-tier celebration cakes need about two weeks. Two- and three-tier cakes need six weeks. If your date is sooner, ask anyway — we sometimes have space.

What size cake do I need?

Our size guide lists approximate servings for every size, from a 4‑inch bento (2–4 people) to a three-tier cake (60–100+ people). If you are unsure, choose “I’m not sure” on the order form and we will recommend the best size for your guest count.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

We can do eggless, gluten-free, dairy-free and nut-free on most designs. Tell us on the order form and we will confirm what is possible. We bake in a kitchen that handles nuts, gluten and dairy, so we cannot guarantee zero traces.

Do you deliver?

Collection from the studio is free and preferred, especially for tiered cakes. Local delivery is available and quoted per order based on distance.

How do I confirm my order?

Once you have approved the design and quote, a 50% deposit secures your date. The balance is due on collection or delivery.
