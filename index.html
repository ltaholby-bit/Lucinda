Our story

A small kitchen, an unhurried approach

What began as weekend baking for friends became a studio built on one idea: a cake should taste as considered as it looks. We keep our order book deliberately small so that every cake gets the time it deserves.

Sponges are baked the day before finishing. Buttercream is made in small batches. Nothing is pre-made, and nothing leaves the kitchen that we would not put on our own table.
