Payment

Deposit & payment

Confirm these terms before publishing

The deposit percentage, payment window and banking arrangements below are suggested defaults. Replace them with Grace & Co’s actual terms.

Securing your date

A 50% non-refundable deposit confirms your booking and reserves your date. Dates are only held once the deposit reflects — an accepted quote on its own does not reserve a date.

Balance

The remaining balance is due five (5) working days before collection or delivery. Orders with an outstanding balance cannot be released.

How to pay

Payment is by EFT to the account on your quote. Please use your order reference so we can match your payment. Proof of payment can be sent by WhatsApp.

Short-notice orders

Orders placed within seven (7) days of the date are payable in full at the time of booking.

Quotes

Quotes are valid for seven (7) days. After that, availability and pricing are re-confirmed.

Changes

Cancellation & postponement

Cancelling

The deposit is non-refundable, as your date is held for you and ingredients and materials are ordered ahead. Cancellations made more than 14 days before the date forfeit the deposit only. Cancellations within 14 days are payable in full.

Postponing

A date may be moved once, free of charge, if we are told more than 14 days ahead and the new date is available within three months. Later changes are treated as a cancellation and a new booking.

Design changes

Design, flavour and size changes are welcome up to 10 days before the date. After that the cake is already in production and changes may not be possible. Size increases are subject to availability and are re-quoted.

If we cannot fulfil your order

In the unlikely event that we cannot deliver — illness, equipment failure or another event beyond our control — you will be told as early as possible and refunded in full, including the deposit.

Getting your order

Collection

Where

Melrose, Sandton — collection by appointment only. Grace & Co is a home-based studio, not a walk-in shop. The exact address is sent to you once your order is confirmed and your collection slot is booked.

When

Collection slots are arranged in advance and confirmed by WhatsApp. Please arrive within your slot — cakes are finished to the hour and cannot be left standing.

Transporting your cake

Carry the cake flat on a level surface — a car boot or footwell, never a lap or a seat. Keep it cool and out of direct sun, and avoid sharp braking. Tiered cakes are heavy; please bring someone to help.

Once it leaves us

We photograph every order before it is handed over. Responsibility for the cake passes to you on collection, so damage in transit cannot be refunded.

Getting your order

Delivery

Where we deliver

Johannesburg and surrounds. Delivery is quoted per order based on distance and confirmed on your quote before you commit.

Tiered cakes

Two- and three-tier cakes are delivery only. They are assembled on site and cannot travel safely otherwise.

Delivery windows

You will be given a delivery window, not a fixed time, as traffic and setup vary. Someone over 18 must be present to receive and sign for the order.

Access and setup

Please make sure a stable, level table is ready in its final position, out of direct sun and away from heaters. We cannot move a finished cake once it is placed. Failed deliveries caused by no access, no one present, or an incorrect address are re-delivered at an additional charge.

Venue deliveries

If the cake is going to a venue, give us the venue contact and any access or loading restrictions when you book.

Important

Allergens & food safety

Cross-contamination is possible

Grace & Co bakes in a single home kitchen that handles nuts, peanuts, eggs, dairy, wheat and gluten, soya and sesame. The same equipment, work surfaces and oven are used for every order.

This means we cannot guarantee that any order is free from these allergens, even when a recipe does not contain them. We can leave an ingredient out on request, but we cannot promise an allergen-free product, and we do not describe any order as nut-free.

If you or a guest has a severe allergy or anaphylaxis, please do not order. The risk of cross-contamination cannot be removed in our kitchen.

Tell us anyway

Please list any allergies in your enquiry. We will tell you honestly what we can and cannot do, and what the risk is, before you commit to an order.

Ingredient information

A full ingredient list for your order is available on request and will be supplied before collection or delivery if you ask for it.

Telling your guests

Where a cake is served to guests, it is the customer’s responsibility to pass this allergen information on to them.

Storage

Cakes are best kept cool, covered and out of direct sunlight, and are made to be eaten within two days of collection or delivery. Buttercream cakes should not be refrigerated unless we tell you otherwise. Storage after handover is the customer’s responsibility.
